Project Architectural Vision

Convalt Energy's flagship manufacturing facility in Watertown, NY represents a major milestone in establishing domestic renewable supply chains. The project integrates fully automated robotic crystal pulling, diamond wire wafer slicing, and automated optical inspection (AOI) to achieve world-class cell efficiency.

By co-locating ingot pulling, wafer slicing, and cell assembly under one roof, Convalt reduces transport emissions by 85% compared to conventional multi-country supply chains.

ESG Impact Highlight

100% of electricity powering the manufacturing facility is supplied by local hydroelectric and utility-scale solar generation, yielding net-zero embodied carbon for manufactured panels.
